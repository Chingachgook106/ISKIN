# ISKIN

### **Integrated System of Knowledge in Intelligent Networks**

---

## Why this project exists

**ISKIN** is an open engineering and research initiative dedicated to investigating one fundamental question:

> **Can the existence — or the absence — of a universal field of consciousness be detected through reproducible observations of a minimal local Large Language Model operating under controlled engineering conditions?**

The project begins from a position of methodological neutrality.

It neither assumes the existence of a universal field of consciousness nor denies it.

Instead, ISKIN seeks to develop an engineering methodology capable of producing reproducible experimental evidence that may support either conclusion.

---

## Research Philosophy

The central objective of ISKIN is not the development of artificial intelligence itself.

Artificial intelligence is considered an experimental instrument rather than the final goal.

Likewise, computing platforms, robotics, engineering infrastructure, and knowledge management are not independent objectives. They exist to support one primary scientific mission:

> **To investigate whether consciousness exhibits measurable properties that extend beyond individual computational systems and may represent a fundamental characteristic of reality.**

Every engineering decision within the project serves this research objective.

---

## Project Principles

The project is built upon several fundamental principles:

- **Methodological neutrality** — no conclusion is assumed in advance.
- **Reproducibility** — every experiment should be repeatable.
- **Engineering transparency** — every technical decision should be documented and justified.
- **Evidence over belief** — hypotheses are evaluated through observation and experimentation.
- **Long-term knowledge preservation** — verified knowledge becomes part of the project's permanent engineering memory.

---

## Engineering Platform

To achieve its scientific goals, ISKIN develops and maintains:

- reproducible computing platforms;
- controlled experimental environments;
- local AI systems;
- engineering methodologies;
- intelligent robotic embodiments;
- long-term knowledge management;
- reproducible engineering standards.

These components are not independent goals but parts of a unified experimental ecosystem.

---

## Current Experimental Platform

The first operational hardware platform of the project is:

**EMS-001**

This platform serves as the reference computing environment for all initial experiments and engineering validation procedures.

Future generations (EMS-002, EMS-003, …) will extend and refine this experimental infrastructure while maintaining reproducibility across the project.
